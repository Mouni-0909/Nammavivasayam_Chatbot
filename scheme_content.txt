
Scheme: Kalaignarin All Village Integrated Agricultural Development Programme (KAVIADP)
Department: Tamil Nadu Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare
Description: A Tamil Nadu government programme for integrated agricultural development in selected village panchayats. It aims to bring fallow lands into cultivation, improve irrigation facilities, increase agricultural production, and develop water resources.
Beneficiaries: Farmers in selected villages of Tamil Nadu, Registered farmer cluster groups, Scheduled Caste farmers, Scheduled Tribe farmers
Benefits: Community irrigation water sources, Solar or electric powered pumpsets, Water conveyance pipes, Farm ponds, Rehabilitation of water storage structures, Water source creation with 100% subsidy for eligible SC/ST farmers
Eligibility: Implementation is in villages selected under the Anaithu Grama Anna Marumalarchi Thittam, Scheme coverage excludes Chennai, Individual benefits depend on the relevant scheme component and beneficiary category
Documents: Specific document requirements must be confirmed with the local Agriculture Department

Scheme: Micro Irrigation Scheme under Per Drop More Crop (PMKSY-PDMC)
Department: Tamil Nadu Department of Horticulture and Plantation Crops
Description: A government-supported micro irrigation programme encouraging drip and sprinkler irrigation for suitable horticultural and agricultural crops to improve water-use efficiency.
Beneficiaries: Small farmers, Marginal farmers, Other eligible farmers
Benefits: 100% subsidy for small and marginal farmers subject to a maximum of 2 hectares per beneficiary, 75% subsidy for other farmers subject to a maximum of 5 hectares per beneficiary, Drip irrigation systems, Sprinkler irrigation systems
Eligibility: Farmers in Tamil Nadu districts except Chennai, Farmers must own or share a water source with pumping facilities, Landowners or farmers with qualifying registered lease documents for a period of 7 years
Documents: Registered lease documents where applicable, Other required documents must be confirmed with the Horticulture Department
Source URL: https://www.tnhorticulture.tn.gov.in/site_assets/site-pdfs/citizen_charter/Citizen-Charter-DHPC-English.pdf

Scheme: Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)
Department: Government of India - Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare
Description: A central government income-support scheme for eligible landholding farmer families across India, including Tamil Nadu. Financial assistance is transferred directly into beneficiaries' bank accounts.
Beneficiaries: Eligible landholding farmer families
Benefits: Rs. 6000 per year, Three instalments of Rs. 2000 each, Direct Benefit Transfer to eligible beneficiaries' bank accounts
Eligibility: Landholding farmer families with cultivable land registered in their names, Subject to government exclusion criteria, Institutional landholders are excluded, Certain higher-income and other excluded categories are not eligible, eKYC is mandatory for registered beneficiaries
Documents: Aadhaar details for official verification, Bank account details, Landholding records as applicable, Additional requirements must be verified on the official PM-KISAN portal
Source URL: https://pmkisan.gov.in/
